Car game · six directions

Tiny Wheels

Six starting points for a small, cute, cartoon car game. Each one pairs a car personality with a world and a core thing to do. Pick one, or mix pieces from several.

Toybox Town

Cozy · Errands

A wind-up wooden car living on a kid's bedroom floor.

Car
Chunky painted wood, peg wheels, a turning key on the back that winds down as you drive.
World
Block towers, rug roads, a pencil bridge, a cereal-box tunnel under the bed.
Play
Run little deliveries between toys before your spring runs out. Wind up again at key stations.

Jelly Drift

Bouncy · Racing

A gummy car that squashes, stretches and wobbles through candy land.

Car
Translucent jelly body that jiggles on landings and stretches into long drifts.
World
Lollipop trees, frosting roads, marshmallow bumpers, chocolate river jumps.
Play
Short drift races. Chain drifts to grow bigger and bouncier, crash to shrink back.

Pocket Island

Chill · Explore

A round bubble car on one tiny island you slowly fill with life.

Car
Egg-shaped two-seater with a glass dome. Unlock roof racks, a trailer, beach tires.
World
One small loop island with a hut, palms and fruit. New bits appear as you play.
Play
Collect fruit and shells, trade with island critters, unlock bridges to new islets.

Moonlight Mail

Cozy · Delivery

A sleepy mail van delivering letters around a hillside village at night.

Car
Boxy little van with soft headlights and a letter on the roof. Honks a tiny "meep".
World
Winding hill roads, glowing windows, fireflies, a lighthouse, owls on fence posts.
Play
Read addresses, find houses, get to know each villager through the letters you carry.

Critter Garden

Silly · Obstacle

Animal-shaped cars racing through a backyard at bug scale.

Car
A frog car that hops over gaps. Unlock a snail car that sticks to walls and a bee car that floats.
World
Grass blades as tall as trees, mushroom ramps, dewdrop puddles, a garden hose river.
Play
Each car has one special move. Courses mix all three so you swap cars to get through.

Cloud Cruiser

Dreamy · Platformer

A little winged car hopping between floating islands on rainbow roads.

Car
Round retro coupe with stubby wings and a propeller hat for short glides.
World
Cloud puffs that bounce, rainbow bridges that appear as you collect stars, sky whales.
Play
A side-view platformer. Jump, glide and boost across gaps to reach each island's sun.

Three choices to make next

Camera
  • Top-down / 3/4 view: easiest to steer, suits islands and towns.
  • Side view: suits Cloud Cruiser and jumpy courses.
  • Behind the car (3D): most immersive, most work.
Engine
  • Three.js: 3D in the browser, toy-like low-poly look.
  • Phaser: 2D in the browser, fast to prototype.
  • Godot: desktop and mobile builds, more setup.
First playable
  • One car, one small map, one thing to do.
  • A 3 to 5 minute loop you can finish.
  • Polish the drive feel before adding content.
